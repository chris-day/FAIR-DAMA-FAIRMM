# FAIR DAMA FAIRMM alignment

This independent local project connects FAIR–DAMA's existing Direct and Indirect FAIR relationships to the FAIR Maturity Matrix v1.2.5 dimensions and personas.

The [16 November source refresh](source-refresh.md) incorporates ontology v1.1.0, document v1.18.0 and workbook V3: 156 activities, 123 DAMA personas, 54 dimension relationships and 127 cumulative level associations from 26 proposed onsets. Big Data & Data Science and Data Handling Ethics remain additional topics, separate from the 11 core knowledge areas.

Start with the [alignment overview](fairmm/index.md), [persona correspondences](fairmm/personas.md), or [persona links to FAIR contributions](fairmm/persona-relationships.md).

The [five high-level connection Sankeys](high-level-connections/index.md) use executable SPARQL over the ontology files, with static, interactive and offline views and downloadable extraction records.

Explore the [Direct and Indirect dimension alignment Sankey](sankey/index.md).

Explore [Tools and Infrastructure alignment to DAMA personas](tools-personas/index.md), traced through activity responsibilities.

The [maturity level Sankey](levels/index.md) shows cumulative knowledge-area association from the earliest supported level through L5. The [level analysis](levels/analysis.md) records the proposed onsets and their source evidence.

The sibling FAIR–DAMA repository is a read-only source. These are conditional documentary alignments; the operational pilot is excluded and no maturity levels are assigned.

The [integrated ontology](integrated/index.md) adds a source-pinned asserted distribution, semantic validation and local recovery checks alongside these documentary views.
